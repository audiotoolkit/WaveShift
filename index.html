Choose the right audio format

Audio formats store sound in different ways. Some keep all the audio data, while others make files smaller by removing some information. Choose a format based on what you want to do with the file.

Lossless and lossy in plain language

WAV usually stores uncompressed audio. FLAC makes audio smaller without losing the original audio data. MP3 and OGG make smaller files by removing some audio information. This is called lossy compression.

Changing an MP3 to WAV does not bring back the sound details lost when the MP3 was made. It only saves the same decoded audio in a different format.

People also ask

Audio converter questions, answered.

Find simple answers about converting audio, file formats, privacy and sound quality.

Is this audio converter free to use?

Yes. You can use the converter without an account or subscription. The audio engine downloads when you start your first conversion. Your audio is processed on your device.

Are my audio files uploaded to a server?

No. Your audio file is processed in your browser and is not sent to a WaveShift conversion server. The conversion engine is downloaded from a public CDN.

Which audio formats can I convert?

You can choose MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG or M4A as the output format. You can try common audio files such as MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, FLAC, OGG, OPUS, WMA and AIFF. Some files may not work if their audio codec is not supported.

Does converting an audio file improve its sound quality?

Not by itself. Converting MP3 to WAV does not bring back sound details that were removed when the MP3 was made. Converting WAV to MP3 makes a smaller file, but it removes some audio data.

Why is the first conversion slower?

The first time you convert a file, your browser must download and start the audio engine. The engine can be reused while the page stays open, so later conversions may start faster.

Can I convert several audio files at once?

Yes. Select or drop more than one file. WaveShift converts them one at a time and gives you a separate download for each file. For large files, convert a few at a time to avoid using too much memory.

Can I use the converter on a phone?

Yes. The website works in modern mobile browsers. Large files may take longer on a phone because conversion uses your phone memory and processor.

What should I choose: MP3, WAV or FLAC?

Choose MP3 for small files that are easy to share. Choose WAV for uncompressed audio and editing. Choose FLAC when you want a smaller file without losing the original audio data.
