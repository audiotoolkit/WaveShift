Privacy information

Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains how the WaveShift static website and browser-based audio tools are designed to handle information. Before publishing, review this policy against the hosting provider, analytics tags, advertising services and any other services you actually enable.

Audio files and conversion

The conversion tool processes selected audio files locally in the visitor's browser using FFmpeg WebAssembly. The audio file is written to a temporary in-browser file system for processing and is not uploaded to a WaveShift conversion server. Temporary files and download URLs are used in the current page session.

The conversion engine is downloaded from jsDelivr when a visitor first starts a conversion. The CDN may receive normal network request information, such as IP address and user-agent data, under its own policies. WaveShift does not control the CDN's logging or retention practices. The site also requests the DM Sans and Manrope fonts from Google Fonts; Google may receive standard request metadata when those font files are fetched.

Analytics and Google Tag Manager

This version includes the Google Tag Manager container configured by the site owner (GTM-TMVGCV79). Tag Manager may load analytics, advertising or other scripts depending on how the container is configured. Those third-party services may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies. The site owner should disclose the actual tags in use and configure consent controls where required by applicable law.

Cookies and advertising

The website itself does not need an account or login cookie for audio conversion. If advertising or analytics services are enabled, they may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. Do not enable advertising until the required privacy disclosures and consent configuration are in place for your audience.

Data retention

WaveShift does not maintain a server-side library of converted audio files. Downloaded outputs are created in the browser. Visitors can clear the page and close the tab to release temporary resources. Browser memory and local download behaviour remain under the visitor's control.

Children and sensitive content

The tools are general-purpose utilities and are not designed to collect information from children. Avoid processing content on shared or untrusted devices if it contains sensitive information.

Policy updates and contact

This policy may need updating when the site owner changes hosting, analytics, advertising, consent tools or other third-party services. For questions, use the contact page. This template is general information and is not legal advice.
