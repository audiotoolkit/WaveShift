Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains how WaveShift handles information when you visit the site and use the audio tools. The site owner should update this policy if hosting, analytics, advertising or other services change.

Audio files and conversion

Your audio file is converted in your browser using FFmpeg WebAssembly. The file is placed in temporary browser storage while it is being processed. It is not uploaded to a WaveShift conversion server. Temporary files and download links are used during your visit.

The conversion engine is downloaded from jsDelivr when you first use the tool. Like other websites, the CDN may receive basic connection details such as your IP address and browser information. Its own privacy policy explains how it handles this data. WaveShift also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic request information when your browser downloads them.

Cookies and advertising

You do not need an account or login cookie to convert audio on this website. If the site owner adds advertising or analytics in the future, those services may use cookies or similar tools. This policy should be updated before those services are enabled.

Data retention

WaveShift does not keep a server-side library of your converted audio files. The files are created in your browser. You can close the tab after downloading your result to clear temporary resources. Your browser controls how downloads are saved.

Children and sensitive content

These tools are for general use and are not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not use a shared or untrusted device to convert private or sensitive recordings.

Policy updates and contact

The site owner should update this policy if the website adds or changes hosting, analytics, advertising or other services. For questions, visit the contact page. This page is general information, not legal advice.
